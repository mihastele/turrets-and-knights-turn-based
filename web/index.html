How it works (Turn-Based Edition)

  • Prep & Combat Turns: Plan your army during the 30s Prep Phase (hidden by Fog of War). Click Ready to start Combat early, or wait for the timer.
  • Soldier Pool: Surviving units return to your Soldier Pool at full HP & Shield after each round and re-deploy for FREE!
  • Protective Towers: Every lane starts with 1 tower per side. All units can attack towers, and ground units cannot pass an enemy tower.
  • Dynamic Economy: Earn Kill Bounties (bigger units = bigger bounties), 10% round interest, factories, and Lane Anomalies (loot, shrines, neutral golems & hazards).
  • 5-Track Upgrades: Specialize units in Attack, Speed, HP, Shield, or Elite Doctrine 🔒.
  • Got a coupon? Redeem it to unlock the Valkyrie, Dragon, Elite Doctrines, and Tactical Abilities.
